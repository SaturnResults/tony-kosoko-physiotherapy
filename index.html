My approach

The person who assesses you is the person who treats you, every time.

For more than three decades I have helped people recover from injury, manage long-term conditions and build lasting strength. My approach is simple: I listen first. A careful, unhurried history is the most important part of getting your diagnosis right, and the right diagnosis is what leads to the right treatment for you.

I am an Advanced Practice Physiotherapist working one to one, so there are no hand-offs and no rushing, just time to do it properly.

Meet Tony

From the stage to the clinic.

My training began not in a clinic but on stage. After an eleven-year career as a professional dancer, I retrained as a physiotherapist, graduating from King's College London in 1995, and brought that understanding of movement with me.

Six years in the NHS followed, working alongside specialist consultants in orthopaedics, neurology, spinal surgery and rheumatology. In 2003 I founded The Practice Centre in West London, and today I work one to one as a solo practitioner, recognised by the MACP as an Advanced Practice Physiotherapist.
