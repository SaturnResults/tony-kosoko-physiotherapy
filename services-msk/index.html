Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

Expert assessment and treatment for pain and injury, from an Advanced Practice Physiotherapist with more than 30 years of experience.

“Listen”

The most important thing I do is listen. A detailed clinical history tells me more than any single test. It is how we uncover what is really driving your pain or restriction, and how it is affecting your life.

Though I see the full range of musculoskeletal (MSK) issues, I have a special interest in:

  • Shoulder problems
  • Spinal issues
  • Sports and dance injuries
  • Ehlers-Danlos and hypermobility disorders
  • Men's health

Whatever you are dealing with, it is rarely an isolated issue, so I look at the whole picture. A correct diagnosis leads us to the treatment your body actually needs, combining hands-on therapy with evidence-based exercise, and, where helpful, a clear plan to prevent it returning.

In a session

What treatment usually involves.

01

Hands-on therapy

Manual therapy to restore movement and reduce pain, chosen to suit your diagnosis.

02

Exercise prescription

Evidence-based exercise you can do at home or in the gym, taught properly so you can do it safely.

03

A plan to prevent it

Where it helps, a clear plan to stop the problem coming back, including strength work.

If it is appropriate, I may refer you to your GP or a specialist consultant for a second opinion or further assessment.
