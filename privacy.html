Privacy Policy

How your personal information is collected, used and protected.

Draft for review

This document is a working draft prepared for the website build. It should be reviewed and approved by Tony, and where appropriate by a legal or data protection adviser, before the site goes live.

Last updated: 2026. Tony Kosoko Physiotherapy Ltd (company number 17176720), registered office 27D Lancaster Road, London W11 1QJ, is the data controller for the information described here.

What I collect

  • Enquiry details. When you use the booking form I collect your name, email address, phone number, chosen service and location, what you tell me about your problem, insurance status and any preferences you give.
  • Clinical records. If you become a patient, I keep clinical notes of your assessments and treatment. This is health data, a special category of personal data, and is treated with particular care.
  • Correspondence. Emails, texts and call notes relating to your care or enquiry.

Why I use it, and the lawful basis

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange appointments, on the basis of taking steps at your request before entering a contract.
  • To provide physiotherapy care and keep accurate clinical records, on the basis of the provision of health care and treatment, and because it is necessary for reasons of professional healthcare obligations.
  • To handle payment and insurance claims, on the basis of performing our contract and complying with legal obligations.
  • To send occasional updates, only where you have opted in, on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Who I share it with

I do not sell your information. It may be shared with your GP, a consultant or another healthcare professional where that is part of your care and you have agreed; with your insurer where you are claiming; and with service providers who help run the practice, such as the form and email providers used by this website. Anyone processing data on my behalf is required to keep it secure and use it only as instructed.

The chat assistant

The website has a chat assistant that answers general questions about fees, appointment times, locations, insurance and booking. It cannot give medical advice, and it is not a way to reach me urgently.

Nobody is watching the chat

The assistant is automated. No one reads it as you type, and it does not alert me. If you describe something that needs urgent attention it will tell you to contact 111, your GP or A&E, but that message is the only thing that happens. Please do not use the chat to tell me about an emergency. Call 999, or call me on 07966 264812 for anything about an appointment.

Your choice before you start

Before your first message you are asked to choose:

  • Start chat, and the conversation is saved.
  • Chat without saving, and nothing you type is stored. The assistant works exactly the same way.

Both options give you the full assistant. Declining costs you nothing.

What is saved, if you agree to it

  • The messages you send and the assistant's replies.
  • A randomly generated reference for the conversation, shown at the bottom of the chat window.
  • The date and time you gave permission.

Your IP address is not stored, and the chat places no cookies or other files on your device. If you choose not to save, only anonymous counts are recorded, such as how many chats were opened and how many led to a booking enquiry. Those counts contain nothing you typed.

Why it is saved

So that I can follow up on your enquiry properly, and so I can see which questions the assistant answers badly and put that right.

Health information, and why I ask permission

Please avoid typing detailed symptoms into the chat. It is not the place for them, and the assistant cannot help with them. If you do, that information counts as health data, which the law protects more strictly than ordinary personal details. That is the reason the assistant asks your permission before saving anything, rather than assuming it. The lawful basis is your consent, and for health information your explicit consent, under Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a) of the UK GDPR.

Who else is involved

For the assistant to work, your messages are sent to:

  • Vercel, which hosts the small program that runs the assistant.
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI model that writes the replies. Anthropic does not use what you type to train its models.
  • The database provider, which stores the conversation only if you chose to save it.

Each is bound by a written agreement to handle the information only as instructed, and not for their own purposes.

How long, and how to have it deleted

Saved conversations are deleted automatically after 90 days.

You can change your mind at any time. To have a conversation deleted sooner, quote the reference shown at the bottom of the chat window when you contact me, and I will remove it. You do not have to give a reason.

How long it is kept

Clinical records are retained in line with professional guidance for physiotherapy records. Enquiries that do not lead to treatment are kept only as long as needed to deal with them and are then deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have inaccurate information corrected, to ask for erasure or restriction in some circumstances, to object to certain processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, contact me using the details below.

Security

Clinical records and personal data are stored securely and access is limited to what is necessary to provide your care.

Cookies

See the Cookie Policy for how this website handles cookies and embedded maps.

Contact and complaints

Contact me at info@tonykosokophysiotherapy.com or on 07966 264812. If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
