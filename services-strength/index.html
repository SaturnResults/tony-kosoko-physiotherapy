Physiotherapy-led Strength & Conditioning

Safe, effective training to recover from injury, with a specialism in post-surgical rehabilitation, taught by a physiotherapist and qualified Strength & Conditioning coach.

I have spent more than four decades weight training, first as part of my professional dance training and during my eleven years of performing, and now as a physiotherapist and Strength & Conditioning coach. I prescribe and teach exercise to help you recover from injury, and specialise in post-surgical rehabilitation, training safely and effectively whether at the gym or at home.

The training sessions teach safe, effective programmes based on current guidelines, evidence based practice and years of hands-on experience. I train people of all abilities and ages, from teenagers to older populations, and recreational athletes to elite sports performance.

How sessions work

Sessions are 60 minutes, available one to one (1:1) or as a shared semi-private (1:2) session with a partner or friend. They run at Westway Sports & Fitness Centre in W10.

Why it matters

The benefits of strength training.

  • Improved bone density
  • Better joint support
  • Improved balance
  • More lean muscle mass
  • Stronger tendons and ligaments, protecting against strains
  • Weight management
  • Mental health, endorphins help reduce anxiety and stress
  • Cognitive health, helps protect memory and guard against decline
