Bupa insurance policy holders

We are happy to invoice Bupa on your behalf although this will not affect your liability as being responsible for the payment of your fees in full. You will need to provide us with your Bupa membership number and a valid authorisation code. Please be aware that Bupa do not pay for appointments cancelled without 24 hours' notice.

Please note that your private medical insurance may not cover all your fees and expenses. Certain treatments and items may not be covered by your insurer, or there may be an excess payable. We will require that you pay any balance if your insurance company does not cover the full cost of treatment. If we receive notification from your insurer that you have an excess on your policy, your funds have been exhausted, or if they refuse to pay any outstanding fees in full for any reason, you will be liable for any unpaid fees in full.

Other insurance providers

If you are not covered by Bupa you will be required to pay at the time of your appointment. We are happy to provide you with a receipt to reclaim this charge from your insurer.

Referrals

If we are referring you on to an expert opinion we will send a referral letter to this third party. You will then be responsible for arranging your appointments and payments. We undertake no liability with respect to the third party.
