Your first appointment

What happens when you come to see me, what to bring, how long appointments last, and how insurance and cancellations work.

Before you arrive

  • Please arrive 5 to 10 minutes before your appointment.
  • Bring clothing that lets me assess your movement, such as shorts, gym or sports wear.
  • Bring any relevant correspondence, scans or information about your problem.
  • Have a think about any questions you would like to ask. It can help to write them down beforehand.

In the room

What happens in a session.

  1. The conversation

    What physiotherapists call the subjective examination. We discuss what has brought you to see me, and I take a detailed history.

  2. The examination

    A comprehensive physical assessment to identify the cause, or causes, of your issue.

  3. Diagnosis and treatment

    Hands-on treatment and exercise prescription based on your diagnosis and evidence-based practice.

  4. Referral, if needed

    If appropriate, I may refer you to your GP or a specialist consultant for a second opinion or further assessment.

Appointment lengths

  • Initial appointments45 to 60 minutes
  • Follow-up appointments30, 45 or 60 minutes
  • Strength & Conditioning60 minutes

Private medical insurance

Bupa patients: please provide your membership number and authorisation code before your appointment. Do check whether your policy carries an excess, as any shortfall in fees is the patient's responsibility.

For all other insurers, I will provide a receipt you can claim back. See the Terms & Conditions.

Cancellation policy

I operate a 24-hour cancellation policy. As a busy physiotherapist I usually have a waiting list, so with more than 24 hours' notice I can normally fill your slot, which means you will not be charged. Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are subject to the full fee.
